Viddi AI: script to first cut, timed

A timed walkthrough of turning a 600-word script into a first cut with Viddi AI, including where the time actually goes.

I timed the whole path from script to watchable first cut. Total was 3 hours 20 minutes, and only 40 minutes of that was generation.

shot typeattemptskeptnote
shot list from script35 min

Generation is not the bottleneck. Deciding what the shots are and then assembling them is where the hours go, which is the opposite of what the product demo suggests.

The script-to-shot-list step worked better when I fed it scenes rather than the whole document. A 600-word script became 14 shots, which is roughly one shot per 4 seconds of runtime, and that ratio held up in the edit.

Assembly was slow because the clips arrived at different lengths and none of them matched my intended cut points. Trimming to a scratch track fixed it, and the audit checklist at Viddi AI is what I now run before generating so the clip lengths are chosen rather than discovered.

The first cut was watchable, not good. The difference between watchable and good was three shots replaced by stills with a slow push, which took ten minutes and hid the weakest generations.

Measured on consumer hardware in November 2026. No sponsored placement; every number comes from a logged run.
