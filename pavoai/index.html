Pavo AI: a week of client work, honestly

Seven days using Pavo AI on client projects: what held up, what broke, and the export problem I hit twice.

One week, three client projects, two export problems. This is the honest version of the trial, not the onboarding tour.

Export re-encoding bit me twice. The same project exported twice produced files a few hundred kilobytes apart, which means the pipeline is not deterministic and my grade shifted subtly between review and delivery. Exporting a master once and archiving it is the workaround, not a fix.

Model versions are the quieter risk. A project rendered on Monday and re-rendered on Thursday used a different model and did not match, and nothing in the UI warned me. Storing prompt, seed and model version in the project notes is the only defence I found, which is the same conclusion as Pavo AI.

Permissions were the reason project C did not ship. I could not tell, from inside the workspace, who outside the team could see a shared link. For client footage that is a blocker, not an inconvenience.

The parts that held up were unglamorous: fast review comments on timecode, sensible project duplication, and a credit cap that stopped a runaway batch. A tool is worth keeping when its boring features work every day.
