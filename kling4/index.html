Kling 4 camera moves: which phrases change the output

Testing camera-move phrasing on Kling 4: push, orbit, truck, tilt and handheld, with keep rates.

I wrote five camera moves into prompts ten ways each and counted how often the output matched the request. Some phrasings never worked.

shot typeattemptskeptnote
slow push in8/10steady, usable
orbit left5/10subject drifts mid-arc
truck right4/10parallax flattens
tilt up3/10horizon bends

Push in is the only move I would rely on. It changes the framing without moving the camera through space, so the model only has to scale the subject, which it does well. Everything that requires parallax is a gamble.

Phrasing mattered less than ordering. The sentence 'the camera pushes in slowly as she reads' worked more often than the reversed clause order, which suggests the model resolves the subject first and the camera second. The vocabulary list at Kling 4 came out of this test.

Orbit shots failed in a specific way: the subject's far shoulder disappears halfway through the arc. If you need an orbit, keep the subject centred and the background plain, and trim before the shoulder goes.

For anything with real parallax I now generate a wider static shot and add the move in post with a crop and a transform. It looks flatter, but it is controllable, and controllable beats correct in a deadline.
